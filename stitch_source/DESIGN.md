## Brand & Style

The brand personality captures timeless mechanical prestige, unapologetic durability, and visceral engineering heritage. It balances the raw, iron-cast soul of a legendary motorcycle with the high-touch refinement of an haute horlogerie showcase. The audience comprises purists, discerning motorcycle enthusiasts, and connoisseurs who demand authentic lineage wrapped in uncompromising contemporary luxury.

The design movement blends **Tactile / Skeuomorphic subtleties** with **Modern Editorial Minimalism**:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep, cinematic charcoal canvas that feels like a private showroom or precision forge at midnight.
- **Surface Texture:** Tactile cues echoing brushed aerospace alloy, matte cast iron, and warm hand-painted brass pinstriping.
- **Tone:** Quiet confidence, authoritative engineering, and restrained opulence without gaudy excess.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from forged engine blocks, vintage badge metallurgy, and machined chassis details:

- **Primary (`#D4AF37` / `#E6CA65`):** Heritage warm gold used strictly for high-impact calls to action, active indicators, and hero brand accents.
- **Secondary (`#C5A059`):** Burnished antique gold for borders, delicate badges, and secondary iconography.
- **Tertiary (`#9E9EA8`):** Brushed silver chrome for secondary metadata, mechanical specs, and technical annotations.
- **Neutral Backgrounds & Surfaces:**
  - `Canvas / Root`: `#0D0D0F` (abyssal cast-iron black)
  - `Surface Tier 1`: `#141416` (subtle elevation, card containers)
  - `Surface Tier 2`: `#1D1D22` (interactive hover states, nested modules)
  - `Surface Stroke`: `#26262B` to `#38383E` (precision gunmetal wireframes)
- **Content Hierarchy:**
  - `Text Primary`: `#F5F5F7` (crisp matte white)
  - `Text Secondary`: `#B3B3BA` (muted steel tone)
  - `Text Accent`: `#E6CA65` (gilded emphasis)

## Typography

The typography system relies on an intentional juxtaposition between high-luxury heritage and technical instrumentation:

- **Headlines & Editorial Statements (`Bodoni Moda`):** High-contrast transitional serifs provide romantic gravitas, evoking vintage luxury coachwork, bespoke engraving, and historical milestone narratives.
- **Body & Long-form Narrative (`Manrope`):** Ultra-crisp, neutral geometric sans that renders effortlessly against deep, dark charcoal grounds, ensuring high legibility across reading distances.
- **Specifications, Badges & Micro-Copy (`Space Grotesk`):** Industrial, mechanical letterforms formatted in uppercase tracking for technical telemetry (e.g., displacement, torque, wheelbase, chassis specs).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system paired with deliberate editorial breathing room:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `gutter: 1.5rem` and outer page `margin: 4rem` to preserve a wide, framed gallery presence.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with `gutter: 1.25rem` and outer `margin: 2rem`.
- **Mobile (below 768px):** 4 columns with `gutter: 1rem` and outer `margin: 1.25rem`.
- **Editorial Asymmetry:** Heavy machinery imagery commands 7-to-8 column focal spreads, while technical spec data blocks and editorial storytelling occupy offset 4-to-5 column rails.
- **Rhythm:** Generous vertical section spacing (`5rem` to `8rem`) mirrors high-end print lookbooks, preventing cognitive clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on generic drop shadows, but rather on physical material cues, subtle illumination, and precise metal edges:

- **Tonal Layers:** Elevation begins at the obsidian base `#0D0D0F`, transitioning to `#141416` for primary modules, and `#1E1E24` for overlays and drawers.
- **Hairline Precision Borders:** Surfaces use 1px metallic borders with linear gradient opacity (`rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.2)` at the top edge tapering to `rgba(38, 38, 46, 0.6)` at the bottom), simulating light striking top-down on bevelled metal.
- **Specular Glows:** Accentuated elements (such as featured CTAs or active mechanical hotspot cards) feature an ambient radial undertone: `0px 12px 36px -8px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.16)`.
- **Backdrop Frosting:** Overlays and fixed navigation utilize a 20px blur with `rgba(13, 13, 15, 0.82)` background, ensuring continuous contrast over high-resolution bike imagery.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture balances mechanical precision with ergonomic curves:

- **Pill Silhouette:** Primary action buttons, status pills, and mode switchers adopt full pill geometry (`9999px`) to echo motorcycle fuel tank contours, hand grips, and vintage instrument dials.
- **Standard Cards & Surfaces:** Standard panels, specs sheets, and modal layers adopt `roundedness: 2` (base `8px`, with `rounded-lg` at `16px`), providing disciplined containment without appearing overly clinical or blocky.
- **Circular Indicators:** Dial indicators, color swatch selectors, and technical hotspots maintain pure 1:1 circle geometry with high-contrast dual-ring borders.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Full pill shape with a brushed heritage gold gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #E6CA65 0%, #D4AF37 50%, #C5A059 100%)`), dark charcoal text (`#0D0D0F`), bold `Space Grotesk` uppercase tracking, and subtle inner highlight.
- **Secondary CTA:** Pill shape with a brushed gunmetal border (`1px solid #38383E`), backdrop fill of `rgba(22, 22, 26, 0.7)`, and vintage silver typography (`#F5F5F7`). Hover states activate an accent outline (`#D4AF37`).
- **Text Link:** Editorial italic serif or all-caps monospace paired with an animated gold underline accent.

### Spec Cards & Telemetry Panels
- Structured with `#141416` backgrounds, framed in 1px `#26262B` borders.
- Top label formatted in `label-caps` (`#9E9EA8`), followed by oversized metric numbers in `headline-md` or `display`, ending with metric unit descriptors in `label-sm`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- Compact pill elements with dark gunmetal fills (`#1A1A1F`). Active states feature a solid `#C5A059` border and glowing gold pip.

### Interactive Hotspots
- Pulsing concentric rings positioned over high-resolution vehicle photography. Outer ring animates subtle opacity fade; central core displays brushed brass icon or spec summary tooltip on interaction.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Recessed `#111114` field background with 1px `#2D2D35` border. On focus, transition border smoothly to `#D4AF37` accompanied by a soft `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)` halo. Placeholder text sits in `#686873`.

### Media Gallery & Color Customizer
- Minimal horizontal scroll indicators with needle-thin gold tracking lines.
- Swatches styled as convex metallic discs with micro bevel highlights displaying precise motorcycle paint finishes (Matte Black, Gunmetal Chrome, Hand-pinstriped Maroon).
